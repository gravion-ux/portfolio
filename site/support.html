Sponsor the open-source tools

Nine tiers, all priced up front. A tier's price cannot be changed once it is published, so these are fixed. Sponsor for the tools alone, or hire me for paid security work — the two are separate and neither requires the other.

Sponsor on GitHub Ask a question first
No account, no telemetry, no upsell. The tools stay free to use whether or not anyone sponsors them. Sponsorship buys maintenance time, not access to a paid tier of the software.

Monthly tiers

Tier Price Who it is for What you get
Supporter $5 / mo Someone who used a tool and wants it to keep existing. Name on the supporters list, monthly build-notes email.
Sponsor $25 / mo An individual developer shipping with the tools. Everything above, priority issue triage, name in all three flagship READMEs.
Partner $100 / mo A team that depends on the tools. Everything above, private monthly office hours, roadmap input, early release access.
Anchor $500 / mo A company that wants a named security-engineering partnership. Everything above, named as anchor sponsor site-wide, quarterly one-to-one, one funded roadmap item per quarter.
Executive $1,000 / mo An organisation with a standing need. Everything above, quarterly review of your use case, two funded roadmap items per year, first response on blocking issues.

Custom amounts are welcome. Recommended amount $25, minimum $5.

Where the money goes

  • Issue triage on reports from strangers, which is invisible work but is what keeps a tool trustworthy.
  • CI maintenance: dependency updates, runner changes, and keeping the pipeline green.
  • Security review of the tools' own dependencies.
  • The next release, including the parts that never make the commit log.

What sponsorship does not buy

No tier grants access to a private repository. GitHub restricts that to organisation-owned private repositories, and this is a personal account — so promising it would be a promise I could not keep. Every tier above is attribution, access to my time, or funded work.

How to sponsor

  1. Open the GitHub Sponsors page and choose a tier.
  2. Pay with a card through GitHub. GitHub charges no fee on sponsorship from personal accounts, so the full amount reaches me.
  3. A welcome message arrives with a note on what to expect and how to reach me directly.
  4. Cancel any time from your own GitHub Sponsors dashboard. Nothing is locked in.
Prefer a one-off? The one-time tiers fund a specific bug, feature, release or quarter. Nothing about them requires a monthly commitment.
