How an engagement runs

  1. Scoping. You send the targets or the diff. I confirm what is in scope and what is out, in writing.
  2. Authorization. Written confirmation that you own the targets or are authorized to test them. Testing does not begin before that exists.
  3. Execution. Read the code path first, reproduce with the smallest possible proof, and stay inside the scope list.
  4. Reporting. Findings with impact, reproduction steps and remediation guidance, structured so engineering teams can route them without re-formatting.
  5. Retest. Critical findings are retested after fixes land.

Frequently asked

Do you need a signed agreement?

A written scope and authorization statement is required. A full services agreement is fine too, and confidentiality terms can be included without changing the price.

Will you test production?

Only with explicit written authorization naming the production targets, and only within the agreed scope. Rate limits and safety guards are configurable.

What if the scope changes mid-engagement?

Additional targets are quoted before they are touched. The published starting prices apply to the scope described on this page.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Engagements are run remotely for clients in any location, with a short scoping call to confirm the target list and constraints.
